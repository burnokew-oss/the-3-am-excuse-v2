One line — the excuse a hardware wallet gives for refusing to sign a
transaction at 3 AM:

> My job is keeping your coins safe from hackers, scammers — and, statistically, you at 3 AM. Come back after sunrise.

The wallet frames its job as protection against three threats, then turns
on the third: the owner's own 3 AM self is statistically the biggest risk
of all. The hour itself is the joke — the line could not be pasted onto a
3 PM transaction — and the device speaks in its own voice, as a guard with
its own priorities, not as a generic assistant.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (pure stdlib, deterministic, no network, no randomness) writes the
line to `/output/entry.txt`. Output: 116 characters, 1 line, UTF-8, within
the 1–200 character gate.
